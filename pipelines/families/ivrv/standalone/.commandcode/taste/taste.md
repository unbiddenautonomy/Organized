# Taste

## Communication & workflow
- Prefers strictly scoped execution: when given a specific instruction, do exactly that and nothing more (explicitly says "do not do anything else"). Avoid unsolicited extra fixes, refactors, or side tasks. Confidence: 0.55
- Explicitly grants broad latitude on model/architecture changes when the stated goal is improving a measurable outcome ("you have all the permission to work with the models and change the architecture") — doesn't want to be blocked on per-change approval for that kind of work. Confidence: 0.55
- Favors the incremental, reversible option first: an online/post-processing layer that ships against the running process, over retraining base models or larger offline rewrites. Confidence: 0.45
