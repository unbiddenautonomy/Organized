What drove the marked result?

Each position counts once. Signals can overlap in time, so these comparisons describe this session and do not establish causation.

Paper trades taken · ₹1,500 stop replay

Replay keeps the recorded entries and replacement exits, with a loss limit of ₹1,500 per trade. P&L uses the first observed triggering price. Earlier exits or extra holding time can affect which new signals a strategy would accept.

Trade P&L and drawdown

Recorded paths use the engine's logged extrema. Tick continuations use retained quotes after the original exit. Minute-close estimates can miss intraminute stops, peaks and drawdowns.
